# Problem Statement

Students may encounter difficulties in internal assessments without receiving timely, individualized academic support. Manual review of marks and course materials can be time-consuming and may not consistently connect a student's learning needs with syllabus-specific resources.

This project proposes an educational prototype that estimates academic risk from assessment indicators using machine learning and provides a syllabus-grounded virtual tutor for structured study recommendations.

The system is intended to support learning and human review. It is not a definitive prediction of student potential and must not be used for grading, discipline, admission, or other high-impact decisions.
